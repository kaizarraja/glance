Glance

Privacy Policy

Last updated 7 November 2026
In short: Glance runs entirely on your Mac. It connects directly to Google to show and change your Google Calendar and Google Tasks. There is no Glance server, no analytics and no advertising, and your data is never sold or shared.

Who we are

Glance is an independent macOS app made by Kaizar Raja. It is not made, endorsed or sponsored by Google.

What Glance accesses

When you sign in with Google, you choose to give Glance access to:

  • Google Calendar: your calendar list, and the events in the calendars you can see, so Glance can show them. If you allow editing, Glance can also create, change, move and delete events and reply to invitations, only when you ask it to.
  • Google Tasks: your task lists and tasks, so Glance can show them and let you add, edit, complete, move and delete tasks.
  • Your email address (and basic sign-in information), to show which Google account is connected.

Glance does not access Gmail, Drive, Contacts or any other part of your Google account.

How the data is used

Your data is used only to provide Glance's features to you: showing your calendars and tasks in the Glance window and in Glance's desktop widgets, and making the changes you ask for. It isn't used for advertising, profiling or training any model.

Where the data is stored

  • Events and tasks are fetched from Google and kept on your Mac, in Glance's folders inside your user Library, so the app and its widgets can show them quickly.
  • Sign-in tokens issued by Google are stored on your Mac in a file only your user account can read. Glance never sees or stores your Google password.
  • Nothing is sent to any server run by Glance or its developer. There isn't one.

Sharing

Glance does not sell, rent, share or transfer your Google data to anyone. Data only moves between your Mac and Google's servers, over an encrypted connection.

Google API Services User Data Policy

Glance's use and transfer of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

Your choices and deleting your data

  • Remove Glance's access at any time at myaccount.google.com/permissions.
  • Signing out in Glance's Settings removes the saved sign-in from your Mac.
  • To delete everything Glance stored, quit the app, delete it, and delete the folders ~/Library/Application Support/TasksWidget and ~/Library/Application Support/com.kaizar.TasksWidget.

Children

Glance isn't directed at children under 13.

Changes

If this policy changes, the new version will be posted on this page with a new date.

Contact
