Privacy

Your calendar stays yours.

There's no Glance server. The app talks directly to Google from your Mac, and keeps everything on your Mac.

  • No middleman. Events and tasks go from Google to your Mac and nowhere else.
  • No tracking. No analytics, no ads, no accounts to create.
  • Google's own sign-in. Glance never sees your password. Remove access any time in your Google Account.
  • Only what it needs. Calendar and Tasks, nothing else in your Google account. Read the privacy policy.

FAQ

Good questions.

The short answers. Anything else, ask on GitHub.

Is Glance really free?

Yes. Early access is free to download and use. If a paid version comes later, early users will hear about it first, and nothing stops working without warning.

Do the widget buttons open the app?

No. Ticking a task, stepping through days, switching Day / 3 Days / Week and paging all happen inside the widget. Glance saves your ticks to Google in the background, and starts itself quietly if it isn't already running.

Why does Google say the app isn't verified?

Every app that reads Google Calendar or Tasks has to pass a review by Google. Glance has applied, and until it's approved Google shows this warning to everyone. Click Advanced → Go to Glance to continue. You can check or remove Glance's access at any time at myaccount.google.com/permissions.

Why does my Mac ask me to approve it?

This early version isn't signed with a paid Apple developer certificate yet, so macOS asks you to confirm the first time. The one-line installer avoids that prompt; if you downloaded the zip, use System Settings → Privacy & Security → Open Anyway.

Does it work with Google Workspace (work or school) accounts?

Yes, as long as your organisation lets you use third-party apps with your Google account. If your admin blocks them, Google will say so when you sign in.

Which Macs does it run on?

macOS 15 Sequoia or later, on Apple silicon and Intel Macs. The tallest widget size needs the latest macOS.

Is anything missing compared with Google?

Year view isn't there yet. Starred tasks, repeating tasks and task due times aren't available to any app outside Google, so Glance can't show them.

How do I uninstall it?

Quit Glance, drag it from Applications to the Bin, and remove its access at myaccount.google.com/permissions.

I found a bug or have an idea.

Wonderful. Open an issue on GitHub. Screenshots help a lot.

Downloading
Here's what happens next

Five short steps. Two of them show a warning: that's normal for an app in early access, and nothing is wrong with Glance.

  1. Drag Glance into Applications

    Open the Glance folder in Downloads and drag Glance onto Applications. The folder also has a How to install guide.

  2. Open it, then click Done

    macOS says it couldn't verify Glance.

    Why: Glance isn't signed with a paid Apple certificate yet. You only see this once.
  3. Click Open Anyway

    System Settings → Privacy & Security, scroll down, click Open Anyway and confirm with your password.

  4. Sign in with Google

    Click Advanced → Go to Glance, then tick every box and click Continue.

    Why: Google's review of Glance is under way. Until it's done, Google shows this to everyone.
  5. Add the widgets

    Right-click the desktop → Edit Widgets… → search Glance.
