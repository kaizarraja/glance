FAQ
Good questions.

    

      
Is Glance really free?
Yes. The beta is free to download and use. If a paid version comes later, beta users will hear about it first, and the beta won't stop working without warning.

      
Why does Google say the app isn't verified?
Every app that reads Google Calendar or Tasks has to pass a review by Google. Glance has applied, and until it's approved Google shows this warning to everyone. Click Advanced → Go to Glance to continue. You can check or remove Glance's access at any time at myaccount.google.com/permissions.

      
Why does my Mac ask me to approve it?
The beta isn't signed with a paid Apple developer certificate yet, so macOS asks you to confirm the first time. The one-line installer avoids that prompt; if you downloaded the zip, use System Settings → Privacy & Security → Open Anyway.

      
Does it work with Google Workspace (work or school) accounts?
Yes, as long as your organisation lets you use third-party apps with your Google account. If your admin blocks them, Google will say so when you sign in.

      
Which Macs does it run on?
macOS 15 Sequoia or later, on Apple silicon and Intel Macs. The tallest widget size needs the latest macOS.

      
Is anything missing compared with Google?
Year view isn't there yet. Starred tasks, repeating tasks and task due times aren't available to any app outside Google, so Glance can't show them.

      
How do I uninstall it?
Quit Glance, drag it from Applications to the Bin, and remove its access at myaccount.google.com/permissions.

      
I found a bug or have an idea.
Wonderful. Open an issue on GitHub. Screenshots help a lot.
